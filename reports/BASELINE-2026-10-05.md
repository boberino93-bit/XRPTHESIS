# XRP Thesis Baseline — 2026-10-05

## Executive conclusion

The thesis begins in a **mixed but genuinely testable** state.

There is credible evidence that XRPL/Ripple infrastructure is reaching real institutional and government-linked use cases. There is also clear evidence that this does **not automatically translate into XRP demand**. The strongest example is Dubai real-estate tokenization: the project uses XRPL, but Dubai Land Department states that pilot investor transactions are conducted in UAE dirhams with no use of cryptocurrencies.

That makes H3 — XRP-specific value capture — the central unresolved question.

## 1. Regulatory position

### SEC / Ripple litigation

On August 7, 2025, the SEC announced a joint stipulation dismissing its appeal and Ripple's cross-appeal. The district-court final judgment remains in effect.

Source: https://www.sec.gov/enforcement-litigation/litigation-releases/lr-26369

**Thesis effect:** supportive for H4 because a major litigation overhang was removed. It does not equal comprehensive U.S. digital-asset market-structure clarity.

### CLARITY Act

On September 15, 2026, the U.S. Senate rejected cloture on the motion to proceed to H.R. 3633 by 49–50. A three-fifths majority was required.

Source: https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/roll_call_votes/vote1192/vote_119_2_00234.htm

A motion to reconsider was subsequently entered, but as of this baseline there is no completed Senate passage.

**Thesis effect:** negative for the near-term timing of the regulatory-catalyst thesis, not a final falsification of it.

## 2. RLUSD scale

Ripple's transparency page reported, as of October 1, 2026:

- Total circulating RLUSD: **$2,509.8M**
- RLUSD reserve funds: **$2,633.8M**

Source: https://ripple.com/products/stablecoin/transparency/

**Thesis effect:** meaningful support for the view that Ripple's regulated stablecoin/institutional ecosystem has achieved real scale.

**Critical caveat:** RLUSD success is ambiguous for XRP. RLUSD can potentially complement XRP liquidity, but it can also satisfy settlement needs that might otherwise have been hypothesized to require XRP. Until measurable XRP-specific demand is observed, this is neutral for H3.

## 3. Dubai real-estate tokenization

Dubai Land Department launched its tokenized real-estate initiative in 2025. Ctrl Alt identified XRPL as the blockchain used for on-chain title-deed tokenization.

Sources:

- https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/news-media/dubai-land-department-launches-pilot-phase-of-the-real-estate-tokenisation-project
- https://www.ctrl-alt.co/press-releases/ctrl-alt-dld

By February 20, 2026, Ctrl Alt reported Phase Two with:

- ten tokenized properties,
- more than **$5M** of real-estate value,
- approximately **7.8M tokens** from the pilot eligible for controlled resale,
- continuing on-chain execution on XRPL,
- Ripple Custody securing the on-chain infrastructure.

Source: https://www.ctrl-alt.co/press-releases/ctrl-alt-dld-phase-two

**Thesis effect:** strong early evidence for H2/H7 relative to a mere announcement, because the project progressed into secondary-market functionality.

### The important counterexample

Dubai Land Department states that during the pilot phase, investor transactions are carried out exclusively in UAE dirhams, with no use of cryptocurrencies.

Source: https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/news-media/dld-launches-the-mena-s-first-tokenized-real-estate-project-through-the-prypco-mint-platform/

**Thesis effect:** this is not bearish on XRPL adoption. It is, however, a direct warning against assuming XRPL adoption equals XRP demand. It is therefore one of the baseline's most important H3 observations.

## 4. What is currently supported

- XRPL has credible production-oriented institutional/RWA adoption evidence.
- Ripple has built a substantial stablecoin product in RLUSD.
- U.S. litigation uncertainty specific to the SEC/Ripple appeals materially decreased in 2025.
- Dubai gives a real example of a regulated/government-linked tokenization project progressing beyond the announcement stage.

## 5. What is **not** yet demonstrated

- That institutional XRPL activity creates material persistent XRP inventory demand.
- That RLUSD growth increases rather than substitutes for XRP usage.
- That XRP is becoming a dominant neutral bridge for institutional tokenized finance.
- That the user's high-price scenarios survive realistic assumptions about effective float, depth, velocity, and persistent net demand.
- That the proposed 6–18 month adoption-to-price lag has predictive power after controlling for BTC/crypto beta.

## 6. Strongest evidence against the thesis

The strongest early challenge is not that XRPL lacks adoption. It is that **some real XRPL adoption is explicitly designed so end users do not need cryptocurrency at all**.

If that pattern scales — institutions use XRPL rails while XRP remains incidental — then the technology/ecosystem thesis could succeed while the XRP investment thesis fails to capture the same upside.

This is why H3 is a gate.

## 7. Immediate research priorities

1. Measure XRP path/routing usage rather than relying on ecosystem announcements.
2. Determine whether RLUSD liquidity creates complementary XRP bridge demand or substitutes for it.
3. Build a production-vs-pilot dataset for major XRPL institutional projects.
4. Register and run event studies on major adoption/regulatory events against BTC.
5. Build a transparent liquidity/valuation model for the user's high-price and sustained-inflow scenarios.

## Baseline state

**Classification:** Mixed / insufficient.

The adoption side of the thesis has real evidence. The XRP-specific value-capture link remains unproven and is the highest-priority test.
